## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-30

### Added
- **Hide Title Bar**: removes the Windows title bar from the Unity main window and fits it to the monitor work area (the taskbar stays visible). Toggle with `Window > Borderless Editor > Hide Title Bar` or `Ctrl+Shift+F11`.
- **Dark Menu Bar**: replaces the light native menu bar with a dark row above the main toolbar, with submenus, shortcuts, checkmarks, disabled items and keyboard navigation.
- Window controls (borderless toggle, minimize, close) in the dark menu row, or in the Unity main toolbar when the dark menu bar is off.
- The empty part of the menu row acts as a title bar: drag to move, double-click to maximize/restore, right-click for a window menu.
